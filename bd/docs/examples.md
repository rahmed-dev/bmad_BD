# Examples & Use Cases

Practical scenarios for using BD: Business Development Suite.

---

## Example Workflows

### Writing a Proposal

**Scenario:** You spot a job posting for a Shopify store redesign with a conversion focus.

1. Activate BDO → *"Let's win some work."*
2. Select `[WP]` Write Proposal
3. Paste the full job description
4. BDO reads it, identifies the client's core need (conversion, not just aesthetics)
5. BDO pulls your past Shopify project from the sidecar — the one where you improved checkout flow and lifted conversion by 22%
6. BDO applies the formula:
   - *What I Saw:* "You're getting traffic but not conversions — the redesign needs to solve that, not just look good."
   - *How I'll Help:* "I've done this exact work for [client type] — restructured the product page hierarchy and streamlined the checkout to 2 steps. Conversion went up 22%."
   - *Next Step:* "Can you share your current conversion rate? I want to make sure my approach fits your baseline."
7. BDO signals: *"This one's strong. Send it."*

### Building the Knowledge Base

**Scenario:** You just finished a Facebook Ads campaign for an e-commerce client — ROAS went from 1.8x to 4.2x.

1. Activate BDO → Select `[PP]` Past Projects → Add
2. Enter:
   - **Client type:** E-commerce / fashion brand
   - **What was built:** Facebook Ads campaign — full funnel (awareness → retargeting → conversion)
   - **Outcome:** ROAS improved from 1.8x to 4.2x in 6 weeks
   - **Skills:** Meta Ads, audience segmentation, creative testing, funnel design
3. BDO stores it. Next time you bid on an ads job, this project gets pulled automatically.

### The "Aha!" Moment — 5 Proposals, Fast

**Scenario:** A productive Saturday morning bidding session.

1. Open 5 Upwork job tabs
2. Activate BDO
3. For each job: `[WP]` → paste description → receive proposal
4. Each proposal:
   - References a different relevant past project
   - Matches the specific client's tone and need
   - Ends with a relevant question
   - Sounds like you wrote it personally
5. 5 tailored proposals sent in 20 minutes

### Writing a Portfolio Piece

**Scenario:** You want to showcase a recent email marketing project on your Upwork profile.

1. Activate BDO → Select `[PF]` Write Portfolio
2. Brief BDO:
   - Client type: SaaS startup, B2B
   - What was built: 6-part onboarding email sequence
   - Outcome: 40% increase in trial-to-paid conversion
   - Platform target: Upwork
3. BDO structures: Problem (poor onboarding = churn) → Approach (mapped user journey, wrote behavioural triggers) → Solution (6-email sequence with conditional logic) → Results (40% lift in paid conversion)
4. BDO suggests specific images:
   - Screenshot of the email sequence map / flow diagram
   - Before/after open rate chart
   - Example email in desktop view showing layout
5. You get a ready-to-publish case study and know exactly what screenshots to take.

## Common Scenarios

**Weak job post:** BDO signals *"Not much to work with here — I've made assumptions. Review before sending."* — you can tweak before sending.

**Formal client tone:** BDO signals *"Client sounds formal — you may want to tighten the tone."* — you can ask BDO to revise.

**No matching past project:** If the sidecar doesn't have a strong match, BDO will tell you and write the best it can — a reminder to add more projects.

---

## Tips & Tricks

- **Add projects immediately after finishing them** — details fade fast; the outcome is freshest right after delivery
- **Be specific with outcomes** — "improved conversion by 22%" is far more powerful in proposals than "improved conversion"
- **Use the platform selector in write-portfolio** — a LinkedIn case study reads very differently from an Upwork portfolio entry
- **Don't overthink the job description paste** — copy the whole thing, BDO filters what matters

---

## Troubleshooting

**Proposals feel generic:**
→ Check your past projects sidecar — BDO needs specific, detailed entries to personalise effectively. Add more projects with concrete outcomes.

**Proposal is over 120 words:**
→ Ask BDO to tighten it — "Cut this to under 100 words, keep the question."

**Wrong past project referenced:**
→ Tell BDO which project to use instead: "Use the Shopify project, not the WooCommerce one."

**Portfolio piece too long for Upwork:**
→ Specify the platform in step 2 — Upwork portfolio entries are shorter than LinkedIn articles.

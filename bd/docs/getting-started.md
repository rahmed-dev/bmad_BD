# Getting Started with BD: Business Development Suite

Welcome to the BD module! This guide will help you get up and running fast.

---

## What This Module Does

The BD module is your AI-powered Business Development toolkit for winning freelance work on Upwork. It gives you:

- **Instant tailored proposals** — paste a job description, get a ready-to-send proposal in seconds
- **Past project memory** — BDO knows your work history and references the right project every time
- **Portfolio creation** — structured case studies with specific image suggestions for Upwork, LinkedIn, or ads

All powered by BDO — your in-house Business Development Officer.

---

## Installation

```bash
bmad install bd
```

During installation you'll be asked:
- **Where to store BD outputs** (proposals, portfolio pieces) — defaults to your output folder

---

## First Steps

### 1. Build your past projects knowledge base

BDO is most powerful when he knows your history. Start by adding your best past projects:

- Activate BDO
- Select `[PP]` Past Projects
- Choose **Add** and enter: client type, what you built, outcome, results

Add 3–5 strong projects to start. The more BDO knows, the better the proposals.

### 2. Write your first proposal

- Spot a good Upwork job posting
- Activate BDO
- Select `[WP]` Write Proposal
- Paste the full job description
- Receive a tailored ~90-word proposal, ready to send

### 3. Create a portfolio piece

- Finish a project you're proud of
- Activate BDO
- Select `[PF]` Write Portfolio
- Brief BDO on what was built and the outcome
- Receive a polished case study + specific image suggestions

## Common Use Cases

**Bidding on multiple jobs:**
Apply to 5 jobs back-to-back. Each proposal references the right past project, matches the client's tone, ends with a relevant question. Five tailored proposals in the time it used to take to write one.

**Building your profile:**
Use `write-portfolio` after completing significant projects. Keep your Upwork portfolio fresh and professional without spending hours writing case studies.

**Keeping BDO informed:**
After winning a new project, add it to the knowledge base. BDO will reference it in future proposals automatically.
